// The Problem

Students are the easiest target on campus.

Phishing doesn't need to hack anything — it just needs a convincing fee deadline and a fake portal. Most students have no fast way to check a link before they've already clicked it.

📲

Spread through trust, not tech

Scams travel through the channels students already trust — class WhatsApp groups, "official" Telegram broadcasts, forwarded emails — so nobody stops to question the source.

85/100

Risk score of a real forward

A typical "exam fee due today" scam link scores this high on our engine — insecure protocol, spoofed domain, urgency language, all at once.

⏱️

No time to think

Urgency is the whole strategy — "pay in the next hour" leaves zero room to verify anything.

🧩

Existing tools aren't built for this

Enterprise security suites protect companies. Nobody built the lightweight, instant, free version for a student pasting a link from their phone.
